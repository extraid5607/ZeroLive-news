About ZeroLive

ZeroLive is an autonomous, high-frequency financial news terminal designed to eliminate market noise, clickbait, and artificial delays for retail traders and financial market participants.

🎯

Our Mission

In modern financial markets, news velocity dictates capital outcomes. Institutional trading desks possess direct feeds from exchange terminals, news wires, and regulatory portals. In contrast, retail investors often rely on mainstream media portals cluttered with invasive advertisements, paywalls, and editorial delay.

ZeroLive was engineered to bridge this gap. By continuously monitoring official regulatory disclosure endpoints from the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) and BSE Limited (BSE), alongside leading global and domestic financial newsrooms, ZeroLive aggregates, categorizes, and streams market-moving developments with sub-minute latency directly to your screen.

⚙️

How ZeroLive Works

⚡ Sub-Minute Ingestion

Our autonomous backend polls over a dozen public RSS streams and direct statutory exchange disclosure APIs every minute, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

🏛️ Direct NSE & BSE Filings

Monitors official corporate disclosure announcements directly from NSE and BSE, isolating quarterly financial results from routine administrative filings.

🎯 Strict Significance Engine

Separates critical, market-altering events (rate decisions, circuit limits, regulatory probes, crashes, surges) from repetitive chatter so you never miss what matters.

📊 4-Country Macro Calendar

Tracks GDP, CPI, Inflation, PMI, Jobless Claims, and Policy Decisions strictly filtered to the 4 core market movers: India 🇮🇳, USA 🇺🇸, Japan 🇯🇵, and China 🇨🇳.

🔔 Native Web Push Alerts

Zero-dependency Web Push notification architecture delivering real-time desktop and mobile trading alerts directly through your browser without third-party apps.

📱 Installable PWA

Full Progressive Web App compatibility. Install ZeroLive on iOS Safari or Android Chrome for a native, standalone, full-screen trading desk experience.
